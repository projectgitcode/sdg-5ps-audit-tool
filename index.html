SDG 5Ps Audit Tool

The SDG 5Ps Audit Tool is a matrix designed to evaluate the velocity and direction of progress across the 2030 Agenda's core pillars in Quezon City. Tailored for the Voluntary Local Review (VLR) process, this matrix aids in measuring trajectory, identifying bottlenecks, and ensuring data-driven decision-making across all districts and local communities.

Instructions

The Tool covers the five (5) pillars of Sustainable Development: People, Planet, Prosperity, Peace, and Partnerships. Each dimension contains five (5) benchmark criteria/considerations aligned with local implementation strategies. Participants will assess the trend/trajectory of each criterion over time within their barangay-/district-/city-level sector using this scale:

  • A: Progressing — Measurable improvement is being made, initiatives are generating positive impact, and indicators are moving toward target completion.
  • B: Stagnating — Little to no measurable change from the baseline; initiatives are stalled, stuck in the pipeline, or failing to keep pace with growth.
  • C: Regressing — Conditions or indicators have deteriorated relative to previous baselines, requiring urgent policy intervention or reallocated funding.
